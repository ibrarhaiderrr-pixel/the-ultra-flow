Need a little help?

Download, installation, and verification essentials.

Do I need to create an account on this website to download?

No account required on the website. Simply download the Windows EXE or Android APK directly from the links above.

Where is my downloaded file?

Check your browser’s downloads or your device’s Downloads folder. Choose the .exe file for Windows or the .apk file for Android.

Can I install it on iPhone or Mac?

The downloads on this page are optimized for Windows PCs and Android smartphones.

Why does Windows show "Windows protected your PC"?

Windows Defender SmartScreen protects against newly released apps. Follow the visual guide above: click "More info" and then choose "Run anyway".

What if Google Authenticator is not listed on sign-in?

Google shows the verification options enabled for the account. Click "Try another way" to choose Authenticator, or contact your account seller for assistance.
